# Volunteer Registration Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow helps collect volunteer information and register interested individuals for NayePankh Foundation activities.

## Information Collected

### Personal Details

* Full Name
* Email Address

### Volunteer Interests

* Education
* Women Empowerment
* Food Distribution
* Social Media
* Event Management
* Technology

## Workflow Steps

1. User expresses interest in volunteering.
2. Volunteer Registration workflow is triggered.
3. User provides their full name.
4. User provides their email address.
5. User selects a preferred volunteer area.
6. Registration confirmation is displayed.

## Output

The chatbot confirms the registration and displays:

* Volunteer Name
* Email Address
* Selected Volunteer Area

## Objective

To streamline volunteer onboarding and provide a structured registration experience.
